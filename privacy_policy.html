Privacy Policy for Hardware Scan All

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Hardware Scan All ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles hardware telemetry, system diagnostics, and device information when you use our mobile and desktop application.

2. Information We Collect and Process

To provide real-time hardware diagnostics, system performance monitoring, and upgrade advisories, Hardware Scan All inspects system specifications strictly on your local device.

  • Processor & SoC Details: CPU architecture, chipset model name, core counts, operating frequencies, and core utilization.
  • Memory (RAM): System memory capacity, available RAM, usage percentage, and virtual swap memory.
  • Storage: Storage partitions, capacity, free space, and drive models.
  • Battery & Power: Battery percentage, charging status, and runtime estimation.
  • Display & Graphics: GPU renderer, VRAM, screen resolution, and refresh rate.
  • Network Interface Data: Local IPv4/IPv6 addresses, subnet mask, gateway, connection type (Wi-Fi/Cellular), and speed.
  • System & Firmware: OS distribution, kernel build, motherboard, and board model.
  • Peripherals & Sensors: Audio endpoints, camera capabilities, and motion sensors.

3. How Your Information Is Used

All hardware telemetry and system metrics collected by Hardware Scan All are processed 100% locally on your device in real-time. Data is NEVER transmitted, stored on external cloud servers, sold, or shared with third parties.

4. System Permissions Requested

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Required to inspect active local Wi-Fi / IP interface configurations.
  • BATTERY_STATS: Required to monitor battery level percentages and charging states.

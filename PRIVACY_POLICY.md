# Privacy Policy for Hardware Scan All

**Last Updated:** October 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Hardware Scan All** ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles hardware telemetry, system diagnostics, and device information when you use our mobile and desktop application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and Process

To provide real-time hardware diagnostics, system performance monitoring, and upgrade advisories, **Hardware Scan All** inspects system specifications strictly on your local device. 

The types of hardware data inspected locally include:
- **Processor & SoC Details**: CPU architecture, chipset model name, number of physical/logical cores, operating frequencies, and real-time core utilization percentages.
- **Memory (RAM)**: Total system memory capacity, available memory, memory usage percentage, and virtual swap memory.
- **Storage**: Storage space partitions, total capacity, free disk space, and physical storage drive models.
- **Battery & Power**: Battery level percentage, charging status (AC/Battery), and estimated runtime.
- **Display & Graphics**: GPU acceleration engine name, VRAM, screen resolution, refresh rate, and display density.
- **Network Interface Data**: Local IP address (IPv4/IPv6), default gateway, MAC address (where permitted by OS), network connection type (Wi-Fi/Cellular/Ethernet), and link speed.
- **System & Firmware**: Operating system distribution, kernel build version, motherboard manufacturer, and board model.
- **Peripherals & Sensors**: Connected audio endpoints, motion sensors, biometrics, and USB device categories.

---

## 3. How Your Information Is Used

All hardware telemetry and system metrics collected by **Hardware Scan All** are:
- **Processed 100% locally on your device** in real-time.
- Used exclusively to render system performance dashboards, metric cards, and hardware health/upgrade recommendations.
- **NEVER** transmitted, stored on external cloud servers, sold, or shared with third parties.

---

## 4. Third-Party Access and Data Sharing

- **No Remote Tracking or Analytics**: We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, user tracking tools, or remote behavioral telemetry trackers.
- **No Cloud Storage**: Your hardware diagnostic data remains exclusively on your device's local memory while the application is active.

---

## 5. System Permissions Requested

To function properly, the App requests standard system permissions:
- `INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` / `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`: Required to inspect active local Wi-Fi / IP interface configurations and throughput.
- `BATTERY_STATS`: Required to monitor battery level percentages and charging states.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address or target anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or request personal data from children.
